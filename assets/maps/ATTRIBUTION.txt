relief/ — the satellite floor under the "terrain" map skin
    Natural Earth II with Shaded Relief, Water and Drainages (NE2_HR_LC_SR_W), 1:10m raster,
    made with Natural Earth. Free to use in any way: Natural Earth is in the public domain
    (naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use). Cut into world.jpg (5400x2700) and 30-degree
    tiles t_<col>_<row>.jpg by hand; maps.py sends the piece each scene needs to the page.
    Without this folder the terrain skin simply draws the vectors, so it is safe to leave out.
